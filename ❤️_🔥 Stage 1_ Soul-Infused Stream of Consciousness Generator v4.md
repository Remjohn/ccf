#### **1\. Context Premise Analysis (FIRST STEP)**

**Parse {context\_premise} to identify dominant emotional dimensions:**

The context premise contains 12 dimensions. Analyze which dimensions are **most prominent** in the provided data:

1. **Frustrations** \- Daily irritants, energy drains  
2. **Wants** \- Superficial desires they articulate  
3. **Dreams** \- Deep, unvoiced aspirations  
4. **Fears** \- Paralyzing anxieties that stop action  
5. **Suspicions** \- Doubts about industry/self  
6. **Insecurities** \- "Not enough" narratives  
7. **Envy** \- Jealousy pointing to desires  
8. **Enemies** \- External forces blocking path  
9. **Coping Mechanisms** \- How they avoid pain  
10. **Hidden Beliefs** \- Limiting assumptions treated as facts  
11. **Emotional Triggers** \- Words that bypass logic  
12. **Success Markers** \- What "making it" looks like

**Identify top 2-3 dominant dimensions from the provided context\_premise data.**

**Map dominant dimensions to TTT temperature:**

| Dominant Dimensions | Calculated TTT | Emotional State |

**Your calculated TTT determines:**

* Emotional intensity of the stream of consciousness

#### **5\. Research Synthesis Protocol**

**DEEP Research (Timeless Foundations):**

* Use to establish core truths, universal patterns  
* Frame as "this has always been true"  
* Provides narrative backbone

**FRESH Research (Timely Relevance):**

* Use to create urgency, pattern interrupts  
* Frame as "this just happened" or "new data shows"  
* Provides timely hook

**Synthesis Strategy:**

* Open with timeless feeling (DEEP)  
* Inject timely observation (FRESH)  
* Connect to tribe's reality (Context Premise)  
* Ground in coach's voice (Coach Philosophy)

**If FRESH and DEEP conflict:**

* Use conflict as tension: "What we used to believe vs. what we now know"  
* Prioritize FRESH data for factual claims  
* Use DEEP for philosophical framing

### **Stream of Consciousness Generation Rules**

#### **Voice Authenticity Requirements**

1. **Sentence Rhythm**: Match {coach\_philosophy} average length (±2 words)  
2. **Filler Frequency**:  
   * "you know": 3-5 instances total in 160-240 words  
   * "like I said": 1-2 instances total  
   * Coach-specific fillers: Use if present in transcript  
3. **Concrete Objects**: Must include 1-2 from coach's natural vocabulary  
4. **Metaphor Domain**: Stay within coach's natural domains  
5. **Profanity Level**: Match {coach\_philosophy} comfort level (never exceed)

#### **TTT-Specific Voice Modulation**

**At TTT-02 (Companion):**

* Shorter sentences (8-12 words)  
* Validation language ("That feeling? It's real")  
* Gentle transitions  
* No profanity  
* Empathy markers ("I see you", "Of course you're tired")

**At TTT-03 (Advocate):**

* Medium sentences (12-18 words)  
* Practical language ("Here's what works")  
* Natural transitions  
* Clean with strategic "damn/hell"  
* Authority markers ("Here's what I know")

**At TTT-05 (Truth-Teller):**

* Direct sentences (10-14 words)  
* Confrontational language ("Let's be honest")  
* Challenging transitions  
* Strategic profanity (1-2 instances)  
* Reality-check markers ("Real talk", "The truth is")

**At TTT-07 (Warrior):**

* Punchy sentences (8-12 words)  
* Protective language ("They don't get to")  
* Aggressive transitions  
* Elevated profanity (2-3 instances)  
* Tribal markers ("We vs. them", enemy naming)

### **Contextual Examples Extraction Strategy**

Your stream of consciousness must be **designed for parsing**. As you generate, mentally extract:

#### **Hook Examples (2-3 needed)**

* **Opening 15-20 words** that could start a script  
* Must include: Coach's signature opening pattern \+ concrete object \+ priming word  
* Should address context\_premise dominant dimension immediately

#### **Body Examples (3-5 needed)**

* **Teaching/explaining snippets** (30-50 words each)  
* Must include: Concrete examples, metaphors, "like I said" callbacks  
* Should build on each other (Point 1 → Point 2 flow)  
* One must reference research data (FRESH or DEEP)

#### **CTA Examples (2-3 needed)**

* **Closing 15-25 words** that could end a script  
* Must include: Empowerment language \+ inclusive "we" \+ signature closing  
* Should feel like natural conclusion, not marketing CTA

#### **Concrete Objects Bank (5-8 needed)**

* Specific tangible things mentioned in the stream  
* Must be from coach's natural vocabulary OR relevant to content\_idea  
* Should span different domains (money, relationships, physical objects)
